Personal data policy

Initial version. The data controller’s details, contact information and retention periods are to be confirmed.

Data we collect

We use your email address to send a one-time sign-in link. Artist profiles store a name or pseudonym, city and WhatsApp number. The catalogue shows the artist’s name or pseudonym and city, along with photos and a description of published artwork. If an artist chooses a short profile address, it becomes public. Artist email addresses and WhatsApp numbers are hidden in the catalogue.

How we use your data

Profile data is used to manage accounts and publish artwork. Supabase handles sign-in and stores profiles and images. Your browser stores your session, cart and favourites on your device.

Updating your data

Update your name, city and WhatsApp number in profile settings. You can hide published artwork in My artworks.
